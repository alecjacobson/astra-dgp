## Final view findings

- **Landing:** left stair, frosted guards, timber handrails, jointed cylindrical column, dark-framed doors and pale piers give a substantially closer spatial reading to the thumbnail. The column and capital remain prominent; the door wall is lighter and less richly detailed than the reference. A broad pale projection above the doors and plain light panels are simplified modeling choices, not matched historical details.
- **East atrium:** ochre brick, restrained stone sills, newly visible dark grille bars, concrete columns, side stair and large glazed entrance establish the strongest recognizable architectural relationships. A close overhead crossing bridge now obscures much of the upper view; bridge placement is inferred and this camera should not be presented as a pixel-matched photograph. No giant light disc or obvious material stretching remains.
- **West atrium:** the curved glass feature, repeated bridges, louvre screens, columns and long axis remain coherent. The close bridge gives a lower, heavier impression than the broad official atrium photographs. Glass is hazier and the interior is less populated/detailed than the references. This is acceptable within the stated approximate scope.
- **Exterior:** the repaired shells and roof junctions present a complete explanatory massing model. Roof slabs, facades, landscaping and adjacent buildings remain simplified. The large circular glazed volume and complete wing dimensions are especially dependent on inference; attractive exterior rendering does not establish accuracy.

## Remaining limits

- The available YouTube material consists of public thumbnail/preview images, not a decoded sequence at 14:52. Do not claim that timestamp's frames were reconstructed.
- Dimensions, footprint, exact stair/bridge placement, curved tower extent, hidden spaces, roof heights and detailed neighboring buildings have not been measured from a survey or full plans.
- The heritage wall, louvres, cylindrical concrete columns, glazing and landing materials have stronger direct visual support than the total exterior massing.
- This assessment certifies only what is visible in the four R06 stills. GLB material differences, browser navigation, geometry counts and download integrity belong to separate technical validation.

No numerical fidelity score is appropriate: camera matching, dates, lighting and evidence coverage differ. Keep R01/R04 reviews and rendered iterations available so the improvements and unresolved assumptions remain reviewable.
