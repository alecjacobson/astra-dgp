## Angle, radius and rise: what can and cannot be used

- **High confidence:** the current repeated 275° continuous spiral with wedge treads is unsupported and visibly unlike the straight runs shown. Clear outer guards have straight edges/segments and distinct changes of direction.
- **Moderate confidence:** two successive runs belong to one inter-floor connection between level 2 and level 3, with a turn around the curved room between them. Use one floor rise split across two runs, not a full 4.2 m rise for each run.
- **Working layout inference only:** a broad U-shaped turn (approximately 180° over the pair) is a reasonable first blocking hypothesis. The available camera sequence does not justify a precise angle; it may be an oblique U with tangential runs and an irregular/widened platform. Do not claim 90°, 180° or any chosen radius as recovered measurement.
- **Radius:** a 4.3 m core radius is a usable retained estimate for initial layout, but the full extent could be oval/arc rather than circular. Fit the observed tangent wall and tread/guard edges first.
- **Rise:** 4.2 m floor spacing remains an estimate. If retained, split approximately 2.1 + 2.1 m and adjust flight riser counts from clear lower/upper footage. Exact riser totals are partially hidden; do not multiply a 25-riser full-storey run by two without checking total rise.
- **Camera motion caution:** apparent direction changes include camera panning/orbiting. The 16:32–16:40 pause alone is not proof of the platform dimensions. Use its surrounding tread geometry and later 22:20/24:00 views as cross-checks.
