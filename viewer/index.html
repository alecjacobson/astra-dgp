About this reconstruction

This editable 3D study combines three public preview frames and the stair-landing thumbnail from APS162: A Tour of the Bahen Centre with official University of Toronto photographs.

YouTube blocked full video extraction, so the exact 14:52 frame could not be inspected. The atrium details have stronger visual evidence than the overall footprint, tower geometry, hidden rooms, and exterior proportions. This is a photo-guided approximation, not a measured survey or photogrammetric reconstruction.

The model was refined through repeated Blender renders and independent visual review. Browser lighting and transparent glass are simplified for interactive navigation. Detailed source notes, editable Blender scene, portable GLB, and render history are in the public repository.
